CREATEBYMOH CINEMATIC WP VIDEOS DIRECTORY
=========================================
Please place your high-definition campaign video (.mp4) files in their respective folders:
- /assets/videos/perfume/ (parfum-gomme.mp4, bleu-chanel.mp4, etc.)
- /assets/videos/streetwear/ (shirt-swap.mp4, hoodie-lookbook.mp4, etc.)
- /assets/videos/jewelry/ (gilded-chain.mp4, gold-ring.mp4, etc.)

The theme automatically triggers and loop-plays them muted natively with custom timelines.
